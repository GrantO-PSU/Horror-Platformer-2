Gameplay loops:
Platforming- Navigating through the environment through jumping, and moving.
Fighting- The player encounters an enemy and uses a sword in order to defeat the enemy which clears the path forward in order to continue advancing the game. 

The assets add to the eerie feel of the game. 

When complete the game will feature more levels with bosses, all the future content should last around 10 minutes. The 10 minutes will including navigating the levels to find gems and fighting monsters to reach the end. In order to make the game more engaging we will add better weapons that the player can find as they progress through the game so that fighting enemies becomes easier.
